## Get help

For setup instructions and product guides, visit the documentation. For bugs and feature requests, use the project issue tracker. For account, privacy, or other support questions, email us.

- [Metakip documentation](https://docs.metakip.com)
- [Report an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/Metakip/metakip/issues/new)
- [Email support@metakip.com](mailto:support@metakip.com)

## When contacting support

Include a brief description of the problem and the steps to reproduce it. Please do not send passwords, access tokens, or private workspace content unless we specifically ask for information through an appropriate secure channel.
