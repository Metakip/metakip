# Home-page feature previews

The feature section reuses the light/dark product screenshots from the official
Features page: `browser-page-*.png` and `invite-access-*.png`.

The additional static previews are frames from existing product recordings in
`public/videos/`, captured at their original resolution:

- `feature-backlinks-light.png`: `backlink-light.mp4`, 0.6 seconds.
- `feature-backlinks-dark.png`: `backlink-section.mp4`, 0.6 seconds.
- `feature-tokens-light.png`: `token-light.webm`, 3.8 seconds.
- `feature-tokens-dark.png`: `token-dark.webm`, 4.6 seconds.

Token previews show access selection before a token is created.
Astro generates responsive WebP versions; these previews do not load or autoplay
the full recordings on the home page.
